# Independent verification of the EPO 2027 calendar

Date: 2026-11-07. Scope: `src/calendars/epo-2027.ts`. Source: [OJ EPO 2026, A30](https://www.epo.org/en/legal/official-journal/2026/06/a30), "Notice from the European Patent Office dated 27 May 2026 concerning the days on which EPO filing offices are closed in 2027", published online on 30.06.2026.

The reviewer was a separate model session (Claude Opus) that did not write the calendar. It received only the calendar file and the URL of the notice. It fetched the notice from epo.org itself and calculated each weekday with the `date` command. It did not read the code, the README or the golden cases.

This review does not set `reviewedBy`. Only a patent attorney may set it.

The reviewer saw the number of closed offices of each row. The office columns above come from the table of the notice on epo.org. The file does not record the offices, because Rule 134(1) EPC extends a period when at least one filing office is closed.

## Findings

- Major: none.
- Minor: the notice writes "All Saint's Day". The file writes "All Saints' Day". The date is the same.
- Information: these days of 2027 fall on a Saturday or a Sunday and are not in the notice: 1 May, 15 August, 3 October, 31 October, 25 December and 26 December. The weekend rule closes them.

## Other checks

- `test/oracle.test.mjs` has its own copy of the 2027 notice, one column per filing office. The engine agrees with the oracle for every document date from 2023-09-01 to 2027-12-31 (1,583 dates: 1,087 due dates, 435 `calendar-missing` refusals, 61 `rule-not-effective` refusals) and for the closure roll of each of the 1,095 days of 2025 to 2027. The counts agree with a calculation by hand: the due dates are the document dates from 2024-09-01 to 2027-08-23; the refusals are the dates from 2023-11-01 to 2024-08-31 (due in 2024) and from 2027-08-24 (the roll reaches 2028).
- `node scripts/mutation-check.mjs` kills 27 of 27 mutants, two of them in the 2027 calendar: a day of only one office removed, and a wrong date.
